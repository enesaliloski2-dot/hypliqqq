Essential questions

Is this an official Hyperliquid or Visa card?
No. Hyperliquid Card is an independently developed concept designed for users of the Hyperliquid ecosystem. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or issued by Hyperliquid Labs or any card network. "Hyperliquid" is used only to describe the ecosystem the concept targets.
Can I get the card today?
Not yet. No issuer or network relationship exists, so no card is issued and nothing is charged. Creating an account gives you the early-access dashboard, wallet connection and status updates.
How will the card work?
The intended flow: connect a supported self-custodied wallet, choose which eligible asset settles a purchase, and pay at supported merchants with conversion handled by an authorized provider, estimate first. Connection is live in your account; conversion and spending are planned.
What will it cost, and will I need to verify my identity?
The proposed price is $5 for issuance with minimal, disclosed fees; the actual schedule, any conversion spread and network costs will be published with the issuer. Identity verification applies wherever law or the issuer requires it; we don't advertise otherwise.
Are the 30 USDT and 5,000 USDT rewards real?
They are proposed programs, not active offers. Neither is funded, and no reward is credited to any account until a backend crediting process and published terms exist. Registering interest records you for eligibility review only.
